# Third-party material

The MIT licence covers the authors' original software contributions. Third-party code, libraries, datasets and annotations remain subject to their own copyright notices and licence terms. No existing notice is removed or replaced by this release.

External Python and R packages are listed in `code/environment`; installed packages and compiled dependency binaries are not bundled. Their licences are not changed by the MIT licence for ACEGP.

The companion data package (`https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23900534`) contains resources derived from external genotype, phenotype, sequencing and annotation sources. These resources are not blanket-relicensed under CC BY 4.0. Consult `DATA_DOCUMENTATION/PROVENANCE_AND_RIGHTS.md` and `DATA_LICENSE.md` in the Zenodo package, or [provenance and licensing](PROVENANCE_AND_RIGHTS.md) in this repository, for source citations and applicable licence boundaries.
