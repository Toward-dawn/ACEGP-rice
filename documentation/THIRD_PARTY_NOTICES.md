# Third-party material

The MIT licence covers the authors' original software contributions. Third-party code, libraries, datasets and annotations remain subject to their own copyright notices and licence terms. No existing notice is removed or replaced by this release preparation.

External Python and R packages are listed in `code/environment`; installed packages and compiled dependency binaries are not bundled. Their licences are not changed by the MIT licence for ACEGP.

The companion data package contains resources derived from external genotype, phenotype, sequencing and annotation sources. These resources are not blanket-relicensed under CC BY 4.0. Consult its `DATA_DOCUMENTATION/PROVENANCE_AND_RIGHTS.md` and `DATA_LICENSE.md`. Exact source citations and redistribution permissions that have not yet been confirmed remain identified there.

If third-party source fragments are identified in the software provenance review, retain their original notices and record their paths and terms before publication. This preparation has not inferred ownership merely from a file's presence in the package.
